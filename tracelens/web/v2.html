HOW TO INTERPRET REPLAY

A score,
with limits.

Flagged means the score exceeds the saved threshold. Below threshold means it does not; it does not prove the log is normal.

Scores are not outage probabilities. These models were evaluated on BGL logs. Other systems need their own training and validation.

Blank lines are ignored. Incomplete trailing groups are unused. The word CNN reads the first 16 word tokens per message. Coverage checks can reject obvious unsupported input, but cannot confirm that a log belongs to the training domain.
